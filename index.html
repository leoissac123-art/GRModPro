How to install, in 3 steps

No computer skills needed. It works the same way for every pack on this site.

1

Download

Pick an add-on and tap the blue Download button. The file is small and saves to your device.

2

Open with Minecraft

Tap the downloaded file. Your device will ask which app to use — choose Minecraft and it imports by itself.

3

Turn it on

Edit a world (try a copy first), open Add-Ons or Resource Packs, switch the pack on, and play.
